# Puhinka - breastfeeding timer and baby journal

A breastfeeding timer and baby journal made for 2 a.m. Everything stays on your phone.

[Open in your browser](https://app.puhinka.com/?lang=en) - Free. Works on iPhone and Android with no store: add it to your home screen and use it like an app.

- **No accounts.** Your records live only on your phone and are never sent anywhere.
- **Works offline.** Optional reminders are the only thing that uses a server.
- **No ads.** No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics.

## One hand, one tap

Start the left or right timer with one tap. Puhinka remembers which side is next and how long it has been since the last feeding.

## Just ask Mecho

Mecho answers from your own records, offline, with no digging through the journal.

## From sleep to a daily routine

Sleep, diapers, tummy time and a daily “Routine” checklist.

## See the whole picture

How often, how long, left versus right: daily and weekly statistics, observations from your own records and a shareable “Our week” card.

## The first tooth

An interactive chart of all 20 baby teeth: mark when each one came in.

## Free

Every feature is unlocked, with no payment and no account.

- **Nursing, bottle and pumping**: A left and right timer with one tap, bottle, pumping and manual entries.
- **Sleep and diapers**: When baby sleeps, when the last diaper was changed and how many wet ones today.
- **Statistics without limits**: Statistics for any period, observations from your records and a PDF report you can share.
- **Growth and health**: Weight and height with WHO curves, vaccines by the immunisation schedule, medicines and visits.
- **The teeth chart**: All 20 baby teeth with the date each came in and notes.
- **Daily life under control**: A daily routine, a milk stash and tummy time in one place.
- **Mecho**: The helper that answers from your own records.
- **The whole family**: Several children in one app: twins or a second baby.
- **Your data and reminders**: Export and import, a full backup of your data and reminders whenever you want them.
